For Contractors

23 Timber Co. supports contractors on land-heavy work across New Hampshire and northern New England. We help you reduce confusion with owners, tighten access and layout decisions, and keep field execution aligned with what the property can actually support.

Access planning

Site & Access Layout

Help with access roads, landings, staging areas, and equipment paths that respect terrain, soils, drainage, and the landowner’s constraints.

We plan routes and landings from LiDAR terrain and drainage data, GIS layers, and elevation modeling, not a walk-through and a guess. That's how we catch a grade that won't hold equipment or a low spot that turns into a mud pit in spring, before it costs you a rebuilt landing or a stuck skidder.

  • LiDAR-based access and drainage feasibility review
  • Landing and staging placement backed by terrain data
  • GIS-mapped slope and soil constraints before machines mobilize
  • On-the-ground walk-through to confirm what the data shows
Habitat-sensitive clearing

Clearing & Habitat-Sensitive Work

Plans that balance what the project needs with what the landowner wants left standing. Especially around cabins, camps, rural builds, and long-term stewardship goals.

Owners are often more particular about what stays than what goes. A single legacy oak or a screen of pine along a sightline can matter more to them than the rest of the clearing combined. We flag that up front so it doesn't turn into a conversation on-site.

  • Define leave tree and protection zones clearly
  • Edge control, screening, and sightline logic
  • Work sequencing that avoids unnecessary damage
  • Practical expectations owners can understand
Owner coordination

Owner-Facing Support

We can walk the ground with the client, translate tradeoffs into plain English, and help set expectations so your crews can just get to work.

Most friction on these jobs doesn't come from the work itself. It comes from an owner who doesn't understand why a decision was made. A walk-through before the first day on-site heads off a lot of that.

  • Pre-mobilization walk-through with owner + contractor
  • Clarify constraints before they turn into conflict
  • Document “what matters” so the field team is protected
  • Help avoid scope drift and surprise objections
Mapping & data

Mapping & Decision Support

Mapping is a tool, not the product. We use LiDAR and GIS data to reduce guesswork on expensive decisions: access placement, staging, drainage risk, and how the terrain will behave once disturbed.

That might be a LiDAR-based access plan like the one above, a GIS terrain and drainage review before you commit to a route, or a quick constraint check on a parcel you haven't walked yet. We're not trying to replace survey or engineering work, just fill the gap before it's needed with data instead of a guess.

  • LiDAR and GIS terrain review tied to field reality
  • Constraint spotting before work begins
  • Data-backed support for access and layout decisions
  • Not a substitute for survey or engineering work

Why contractors bring us in

Land-heavy projects fail for predictable reasons: access gets guessed, owners don't understand tradeoffs, and small decisions cascade into rework.

  • Single point of contact who understands owners and field crews
  • Better access and staging decisions before machines cost money
  • Clear constraints so your team isn’t the bad guy on site
  • Fewer surprises, fewer change conversations, cleaner execution

We're not here to slow your job down. We're here to prevent the avoidable problems that do.
