For Land Owners

We work with landowners who want to get more out of their property. Better hunting, healthier timber, a clearer picture of what they own, and a plan worth following through on. It starts with walking the property and having an honest conversation about what's realistic.

If you're buying land

A property can look right on a walk-through and still have problems that don't show up until after closing. Species composition that doesn't match what you were told, drainage issues that limit access, timber that's been high-graded without disclosure.

We use LiDAR and aerial analysis to give you a clear picture of what you're actually buying before you commit. Canopy structure, terrain, drainage, access feasibility, and realistic timber value. All reviewed and delivered in plain language so you can make a confident decision or negotiate from a stronger position.

  • LiDAR-backed species and canopy structure analysis
  • Terrain, drainage, and access feasibility review
  • Timber condition and realistic value assessment
  • Clear deliverable before closing — not after

If you hunt your land

Most properties decline slowly and the owner doesn't notice until the hunting gets noticeably worse. Pressure accumulates, access routes that made sense early on start educating deer, and habitat that looked promising never gets developed into something that actually holds animals.

We look at how wildlife is using your ground right now and how your current access and hunting pressure is affecting that. Bedding, movement, food, water. From there we build a layout that accounts for wind, pressure distribution, and long-term habitat improvement. Not just where to hang a stand this season.

  • Access and entry/exit planning tied to wind and pressure
  • Stand placement based on real movement patterns
  • Bedding, security cover, and food source relationships
  • Layouts designed to hold up after years of real use

If you care about timber value

A lot of New England woodlots have been selectively cut or left alone long enough that someone else's decisions are already shaping what the land is worth — and what it will be worth in twenty years. Sometimes that's fine. Often it isn't, and the owner doesn't know the difference until it's too late to change course.

We assess stand conditions, regeneration, and species composition so you understand what you actually have. Then we help you figure out what decisions matter now, what can wait, and how to improve forest health without trading future harvest value for short-term results.

  • Stand condition and regeneration assessment
  • TSI strategy tied to future harvest options
  • Access planning that protects soils and long-term growth
  • Coordination with foresters or loggers when appropriate

If you're thinking long-term

Stewardship planning is less about doing everything and more about doing things in the right order. Early decisions have a way of closing off later options — access built in the wrong place, timber cut before regeneration is established, habitat work that conflicts with future income goals.

We help you get clarity on what to do first, what to hold off on, and why the sequence matters. Plans are phased and realistic — built around what you can actually execute over time, not what looks good on paper.

  • Multi-year stewardship planning with phased priorities
  • Habitat, timber, and access decisions integrated together
  • Realistic timelines and capital sequencing
  • Plans that adapt as conditions and goals change

What to expect

Every engagement starts the same way — walking the property and talking through your goals, your constraints, and what you've already tried. From there we identify current conditions, future opportunities, and the sequence that makes the most sense given your timeline and budget.

Where it makes sense, we also act as your representative through execution — coordinating qualified subcontractors, keeping work aligned with the plan, and making sure decisions made in the field don't create problems later.

The goal isn't to sell you more work. It's to help you make better decisions with what you already own.
